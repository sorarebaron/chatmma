UFC 323 Predictions: Yan vs Dvalishvili
By Alexander K. Lee - MMA Fighting

The UFC returns to Las Vegas with UFC 323, featuring a bantamweight rematch between Petr Yan and Merab Dvalishvili in the main event.

Main Event: Petr Yan vs Merab Dvalishvili

I'm going with Petr Yan in this rematch. While Merab had an incredible 2025 run, I think Yan's improved takedown defense and counter-striking will be the difference. Yan has been working extensively on his wrestling, and I believe he'll be able to stuff most of Merab's takedown attempts. I'm picking Yan by unanimous decision.

Co-Main Event: Joshua Van vs Alexandre Pantoja

This is a tough one, but I'm leaning towards Joshua Van. He's got explosive power and Pantoja has been taking damage in recent fights. I think Van catches him early. Van by KO in round 1.

Tatsuro Taira vs Brandon Moreno

Taira has looked incredible, and Moreno is on the decline. I'm picking Taira by knockout in round 2. His striking has improved dramatically.

Payton Talbott vs Henry Cejudo

Cejudo is past his prime, but his experience will matter here. However, Talbott is the future. I'm going with Talbott by unanimous decision.

Jan Blachowicz vs Bogdan Guskov

This should be a competitive fight. I'm picking Blachowicz by split decision, but it could go either way.

DFS Notes:
- Van is my top play for knockout upside
- Taira is a great value at his price point
- Talbott should provide solid points even in a decision
